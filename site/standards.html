Built to recognized standards

For each standard, Michael Cunningham mapped every requirement to what the site actually does, with links to the code, configuration or test that proves it. Where the site falls short, or a one-person company can't show what a large one would, the write-up says so plainly. These are self-assessments, not audits or certifications.

  • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026 · PDF
    Each of the ten AI risks, such as prompt injection, sensitive data disclosure and excessive agency, and the control that answers it.
    8 met 1 partly met 1 not applicable
  • OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026 · PDF
    The risks of AI that uses tools and takes actions, from goal hijack to rogue agents, and why both of the site's AI agents have agency without authority.
    8 met 1 partly met 1 not applicable
  • MITRE ATLAS · PDF
    The attacker techniques that apply to the site's AI, from prompt injection to cost harvesting, and the defense against each.
    11 met
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework · PDF
    How the AI chatbot's risks are governed, mapped, measured and managed, across all 19 categories.
    14 met 5 partly met
  • NIST AI 600-1: Generative AI Profile · PDF
    The twelve risks NIST names for generative AI, from confabulation to value chain risk, and how each is handled.
    8 met 4 partly met
  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023 · PDF
    The AI management system behind the AI chatbot: clauses 4 to 10 and the Annex A controls.
    13 met 3 partly met
  • ISO/IEC 23894:2023 · PDF
    AI risk management: how the site's AI risks are identified, analyzed, treated and monitored, and the AI-specific risk sources.
    14 met 4 partly met
  • ISO/IEC 42005:2025 · PDF
    An AI system impact assessment of the AI chatbot and the AI SOC: intended use, misuse, data, benefits, harms and residual risk.
    8 met 2 partly met
  • EU AI Act · PDF
    Where the AI chatbot sits in the EU's risk tiers, and the transparency duties that apply.
    3 met 1 partly met 3 not applicable
  • Colorado AI laws: SB 26-189 and HB 26-1263 · PDF
    Colorado's automated decision law, which replaced the Colorado AI Act, and its new chatbot law, both taking effect January 1, 2027.
    2 met 3 partly met 3 not applicable
  • Model Risk Management: SR 26-2 (formerly SR 11-7) · PDF
    The US banking agencies' model risk guidance, which now leaves generative AI out, applied to the site's AI the way a bank would.
    4 met 5 partly met 1 not applicable
  • PCI DSS v4.0.1 · PDF
    All twelve requirements for the store and support chat, starting with the biggest control: keeping card data out.
    8 met 4 partly met
  • SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria · PDF
    A readiness review against the criteria a SOC 2 auditor tests, CC1 to CC9 plus the optional categories.
    10 met 3 partly met
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 · PDF
    The information security management system: clauses 4 to 10 and all four themes of the Annex A controls.
    10 met 6 partly met 2 not applicable

Each write-up is also in the code repository, in docs/standards.
