Built to recognized standards

For each standard, Michael Cunningham mapped every requirement to what the site actually does, with links to the code, configuration or test that proves it. Where the site falls short, or a one-person company can't show what a large one would, the write-up says so plainly. These are self-assessments, not audits or certifications.

  • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026 · PDF
    Each of the ten AI risks, such as prompt injection, sensitive data disclosure and excessive agency, and the control that answers it.
    8 met 1 partly met 1 not applicable
  • OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026 · PDF
    The risks of AI that uses tools and takes actions, from goal hijack to rogue agents, and why both of the site's AI agents have agency without authority.
    8 met 1 partly met 1 not applicable
  • MITRE ATLAS · PDF
    The attacker techniques that apply to the site's AI, from prompt injection to cost harvesting, and the defense against each.
    11 met
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework · PDF
    How Tucker's risks are governed, mapped, measured and managed, across all 19 categories, with NIST's Generative AI Profile.
    14 met 5 partly met
  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023 · PDF
    The AI management system behind Tucker: clauses 4 to 10 and the Annex A controls.
    13 met 3 partly met
  • ISO/IEC 23894:2023 · PDF
    AI risk management: how the site's AI risks are identified, analyzed, treated and monitored, and the AI-specific risk sources.
    14 met 4 partly met
  • ISO/IEC 42005:2025 · PDF
    An AI system impact assessment of Tucker and the AI SOC Squirrel: intended use, misuse, data, benefits, harms and residual risk.
    8 met 2 partly met
  • EU AI Act · PDF
    Where Tucker sits in the EU's risk tiers, and the transparency duties that apply.
    3 met 1 partly met 3 not applicable
  • PCI DSS v4.0.1 · PDF

    10 met 3 partly met
