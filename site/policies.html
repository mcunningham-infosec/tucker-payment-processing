AI Policies

Michael wrote the company's policies first, then built the AI to follow them. Tucker answers policy questions from these exact documents and names his source, and the guardrails in the code enforce what they promise.

  • AI Assistant Use Policy
    What Tucker, our AI assistant, may and may not do, and the guardrails around him.
  • Card Security and Privacy Policy

  • Refunds and Disputes Policy
    Refunds, disputes and declined transactions.
  • Data Retention Policy
    What the site keeps, for how long, and the cookies it uses.

Built to recognized standards

  • PCI DSS v4.0: no card data ever entered or exposed, tokens and last 4 digits only, server-side pricing, least-privilege database roles, strong HTTPS and logging.
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0): how Tucker's risks are governed, mapped, measured and managed, including NIST's Generative AI Profile.
  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023: the AI management system behind Tucker: policy, roles, risk assessment, controls and review.
  • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025): each LLM risk, such as prompt injection, sensitive data disclosure and excessive agency, and the guardrail that answers it.
  • EU AI Act (transparency): Tucker always discloses that he is an AI.
