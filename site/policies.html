Built to recognized standards

For each standard, every requirement is mapped to what the site actually does, with links to the code that proves it and honest notes where it falls short. See all nine standards.

  • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026: each AI risk, such as prompt injection, sensitive data disclosure and excessive agency, and the control that answers it.
  • OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026: the risks of AI that uses tools and takes actions, and why both of the site's AI agents can choose what to look at but never change anything.
  • MITRE ATLAS: the attacker techniques that apply to the site's AI, and the defense against each.
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework: how Tucker's risks are governed, mapped, measured and managed, including NIST's Generative AI Profile.
  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023: the AI management system behind Tucker: policy, roles, risk assessment, controls and review.
  • ISO/IEC 42005:2025: an AI impact assessment of Tucker and the AI SOC Squirrel, in the standard's structure.
  • EU AI Act: where Tucker sits in the risk tiers, and the transparency duties that apply.
  • PCI DSS v4.0.1: no card data ever entered or exposed, tokens and last 4 digits only, server-side pricing, least-privilege database roles, strong HTTPS and logging.
  • SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria: a readiness review against the criteria a SOC 2 auditor tests.
