# Refunds and Disputes Policy
Tucker Payment Processing (fictional) - Policy TPP-OPS-02

## Refunds
Refunds are issued by the merchant, not by Tucker Payment Processing. Once a merchant issues a refund, it usually appears on the customer's account within 5 to 10 business days.

## Disputes
If a customer does not recognize a charge or did not receive what they paid for, they can open a dispute within 120 days of the transaction date. Disputes are reviewed by our disputes team, usually within 30 days. While a dispute is open, the transaction shows the status "disputed".

## Declined transactions
Common decline reasons include insufficient funds, a frozen card, an expired card, or a fraud check that needs verification. Support can explain why a transaction was declined, but cannot approve or retry a declined transaction.

## What support cannot do
Support agents and Tucker cannot issue refunds, reverse charges, move money, or change transaction records. These actions require the secure account portal or the disputes team.
