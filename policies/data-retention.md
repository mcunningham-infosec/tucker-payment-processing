# Data Retention Policy
Tucker Payment Processing (fictional) - Policy TPP-PRV-03

## Website visit logs
Visit logs (time, IP address, pages viewed) are kept for 30 days to understand how the demonstration is used, then automatically deleted. They are never sold or shared.

## Chat conversations
The text of chats with Tucker is not stored with visitor IP addresses. Guardrail events (for example, "prompt injection blocked") are recorded without the chat text so we can report how the guardrails perform.

## Cookies
The site uses one strictly necessary session cookie to keep a chat and the Treat Shop cart together while you browse. It expires when you close your browser. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

## Customer data
All customer, card, and transaction data on this site is fictional and exists only to demonstrate the technology.
